Our Leadership Team

Meet the driving forces behind Aura-X. Discover the core visionaries shaping the platform's strategic direction, technical growth, and day-to-day operations.

Ujjwal Mehta

Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

As the Founder and CEO of Aura-X, Ujjwal Mehta is the primary architect of the platform's overarching vision, culture, and long-term strategic trajectory. Recognizing the need for a more personalized, creative, and community-driven social ecosystem, he conceptualized Aura-X to bridge the gap between human connection and modern digital technology.

Key Responsibilities & Contributions:

  • Strategic Vision & Direction: Defining the core mission of Aura-X, ensuring every feature aligns with the goal of empowering creators, communities, and everyday users.
  • Global Ecosystem Growth: Spearheading high-level growth initiatives, partnership opportunities, and expansion planning as the platform scales worldwide.
  • Product Innovation: Working closely with product and development teams to guide new feature releases, user privacy standards, and next-generation social tools.

Ansh Thakur

General Project Manager

As the General Project Manager at Aura-X, Ansh Thakur serves as the operational backbone of the organization. He is responsible for translating the company's grand vision into actionable execution, managing project milestones, streamlining workflow efficiency, and ensuring that development timelines, technical deployments, and brand initiatives run seamlessly.

Key Responsibilities & Contributions:

  • Operations & Milestone Execution: Managing day-to-day project workflows, tracking platform development phases, and ensuring that every product release meets rigorous quality standards.
  • Cross-Functional Coordination: Acting as the central bridge between design, development, and support frameworks to maintain unified momentum across all platform features.
  • Platform Performance & Infrastructure: Overseeing technical maintenance, user feedback integration, and digital infrastructure optimization to guarantee a smooth experience for the entire Aura-X community.
